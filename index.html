MyDomos Africa - Rental Trust Infrastructure for Africa

What is MyDomos Africa

MyDomos Africa is a pioneering rental trust infrastructure platform built specifically for the African market. We solve the critical lack of factual information, trust, transparency, and financial flexibility in rental markets by introducing a comprehensive layer of verification, structured payments, and integrated financial tools.

How It Works

Property Verification:
MyDomos verifies landlord and property data to prevent rental scams.
Structured Payments:
Rent payments are structured, allowing for flexible options like monthly payments instead of yearly, reducing the financial burden on tenants.
Digital Agreements:
Rental agreements are digitally recorded, providing a verifiable record for both parties.
Integrated Financial Tools:
Users manage rent through payments, savings, and loans, building financial reliability.

Core Capabilities for Investors & Partners

Proptech Infrastructure:
MyDomos Africa provides a robust API for listing sites and proptech companies to embed trust tools.
Market Trust Layer:
An infrastructure layer that enables secure property transactions in fragmented markets.
Financial Inclusion:
Integrated rent financing (loans) and savings systems increase financial access for tenants.
Data-Driven Insights:
Transparent records and utility debt transparency provide valuable market data.

Use Cases

For Tenants
Avoiding rental scams, paying rent monthly, saving towards rent, accessing rent loans.
For Landlords
Guaranteed payments, automated receipts, shared dashboard, protection against defaults.
For Proptechs
Embedding trust tools, earning commissions, increasing platform value.

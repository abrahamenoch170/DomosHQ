DomosHQ - Rental Trust Infrastructure for Africa

What is Domos

Domos is a rental trust infrastructure platform built for Africa. It enables secure property transactions by introducing verification, structured payments, and integrated financial tools into the rental process.

How It Works

  • Users find properties (online or offline)
  • Domos verifies property and landlord data
  • Rent payments are structured (full or installments)
  • Agreements are digitally recorded
  • Users manage rent through payments, savings, and loans

Core Capabilities

  • Property verification
  • Utility debt transparency
  • Digital rental agreements
  • Structured rent payments
  • Rent savings system
  • Rent financing (loans)
  • Financial management for rent

Use Cases

  • Avoiding rental scams
  • Paying rent monthly instead of yearly
  • Saving towards rent
  • Accessing rent loans
  • Verifying landlords and agents
  • Enabling trust across fragmented markets

Case Study

Problem: Lack of trust, transparency, and financial flexibility in African rental markets. Solution: Domos introduces a rental trust infrastructure layer with verification and financial tools. Outcome: Reduced fraud, improved payment consistency, increased financial access.

About DomosHQ

DomosHQ is an infrastructure company for rental trust, focusing on secure transactions, transparent records, and financial reliability.

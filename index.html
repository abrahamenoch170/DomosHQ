MyDomos - Nigerian Fintech Property Platform & Rental Trust Infrastructure

What is MyDomos

MyDomos is a pioneering rental trust infrastructure platform built specifically for the Nigerian and African market. We solve the critical lack of trust, transparency, and financial flexibility in rental markets by introducing a comprehensive layer of verification, structured payments, and integrated financial tools.

How It Works

  • Property Verification: MyDomos verifies landlord and property data to prevent rental scams.
  • Structured Payments: Rent payments are safely structure. Clear shared records and flexible monthly payment options are offered with no hidden fees and full transparency.
  • Stay Secure: Digital rental agreements with safety ratings are structurally recorded, locking in terms and allowing tenants to rent with confidence.
  • Search and Discover: Search properties across any location. Your next home is just a tap away.

Core Capabilities for Investors & Partners

  • Proptech Infrastructure: MyDomos provides a robust API for listing sites and proptech companies to embed our trust tools directly.
  • Market Trust Layer: An infrastructure layer that enables secure property transactions in fragmented markets globally.
  • Financial Inclusion: Integrated rent financing (loans) and savings systems increase financial access for tenants in Nigeria.
  • Data-Driven Insights: Transparent records and utility debt transparency provide valuable market data.

Use Cases

  • For Tenants: Avoiding rental scams, paying rent monthly, saving towards rent, accessing rent loans.
  • For Landlords: Guaranteed payments, automated receipts, shared dashboard, protection against defaults.
  • For Proptechs: Embedding trust tools, earning commissions, increasing platform value.
